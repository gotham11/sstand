Our Story

Built to Get You Results

Stanford O'quv Markazi was founded in 2015 with one goal: give every student in Uzbekistan access to world-class English education. We started as a small, passionate team in Namangan — today we're the city's most recognized exam preparation centre.

We specialize in IELTS, CEFR, DTM preparation and pre-school English. Our methodology blends proven international frameworks with a deep understanding of what Uzbek learners specifically need.

As an official IDP IELTS & British Council partner, we meet the highest global teaching standards — and our results prove it.
